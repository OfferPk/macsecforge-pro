# MACsecForge Pro \- Commercialization & Monetization Playbook

## 1\. Product Market Fit & Target Customer Persona

### Primary Audience:

- **Cloud & Data Center Interconnect (DCI) Architects**: Connecting AWS DirectConnect, Azure ExpressRoute, and Google Cloud Dedicated Interconnect with 100G/400G line-rate MACsec.  
- **Financial HFT & Banking Infrastructure Engineers**: Regulated under PCI-DSS v4.0 and SEC/FINRA to encrypt all off-premises dark fiber and metro optical links at Layer 2\.  
- **Enterprise Network Security & Defense Contractors**: Complying with DoD / CNSA / CMMC zero-trust cryptographic requirements.  
- **Telecom Service Providers & ISP Backbone Operators**: Securing carrier Ethernet transit (E-Line / E-LAN) links without sacrificing line-rate throughput.

### Core Value Proposition:

"Prevent catastrophic high-speed packet number rollover drops, MTU blackholes, and unencrypted cleartext leaks across 100G/400G/800G optical links before touching production routers."
